Clicking a dungeon header icon attempts to teleport to that dungeon when teleport is unlocked.
Teleport spell mappings live on each dungeon entry in the active season in season-config.lua.
Teleport uses the player's unlocked dungeon teleport spell for that map.
Hovering a dungeon header tooltip shows whether teleport is available, unlocked, or unavailable.
The addon shows a message when teleport to a dungeon is not unlocked.
The addon shows a message when no teleport spell is mapped for a dungeon.
