When a new player joins your party, a small alert may appear if they have keys-history votes or saved notes.
The alert does not appear for the local player.
The alert does not appear on the initial roster snapshot after login or reload.
The alert does not appear in raids.
The alert shows the player's name (with realm when known) in class color.
The alert shows an overall vote score where check = +1, neutral = 0, and x = -1.
The alert shows a breakdown of check, neutral, and x counts.
The alert lists up to four saved notes, with a count of any remaining notes.
The alert has an All notes button that opens the full player notes view.
The alert can be moved by dragging the window.
The alert closes when you click the close button.
If multiple joiners qualify at once, alerts are queued and shown one at a time.
