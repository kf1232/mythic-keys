The party list opens when you run `/keyf`.
The party list closes when you run `/keyf` again while it is open.
The party list closes when you click the close button.
The party list can be moved by dragging the window.
The party list shows five empty member rows and one column per season dungeon.
The party list shows dungeon icons and short labels in the column headers.
The party list shows `?` and `+` note buttons on the far right of each row.
The party list does not load roster, keystone, or season-best data.
